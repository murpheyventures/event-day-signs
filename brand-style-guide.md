# Event Day Signs — Brand & Style Guide

## Brand idea

Event Day Signs helps spectators show up loudly for the people they love on race day. The brand should feel energetic, generous, and impossible to miss—like a great sign on the course.

## Audience

Friends, family members, teammates, and supporters attending marathons, triathlons, cycling events, 5Ks, and 10Ks.

## Voice

- Energetic: use active verbs and race-day momentum.
- Motivational: make the recipient feel seen and supported.
- Personal: speak to the specific racer, team, or moment.
- Clear: keep product names and calls to action easy to scan.

Suggested language: “Cheer louder.” “Make their mile.” “Big signs for big moments.” “Your favorite racer will see this.”

Avoid corporate, overly polished, or defeatist language. Humor can be bold, but it should feel supportive rather than mean-spirited.

## Color palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary purple | Race-day purple | `#6A20D9` |
| Teal | Runner teal | `#00AFD0` |
| Orange | Action orange | `#FF541F` |
| Yellow | Finish-line yellow | `#FFC51A` |
| Ink | Deep ink | `#17122F` |
| Paper | Warm white | `#FFFDF8` |

Use purple and teal for identity and navigation, orange for action moments, and yellow for highlights, badges, and emphasis. Keep text on light surfaces in deep ink for readability.

## Typography

- Display headings: bold, athletic sans-serif with tight tracking.
- Body text: Trebuchet MS or a similar friendly sans-serif.
- Labels: bold uppercase with generous letter spacing.
- Avoid delicate serif typography, thin weights, and long all-caps paragraphs.

## Logo use

Use the full Event Day Signs logo in the header, social profiles, and branded documents. Keep clear space around the sign and do not stretch, recolor, add effects, or place it on a busy background. The simplified teal runner favicon is for small digital placements only.

## Social media direction

Use high-contrast blocks of purple, teal, orange, and yellow. Lead with the racer or the sign message, then add a short supporting line. Captions should feel like a friend cheering from the sideline: specific, warm, and a little loud.

Example caption: “They trained for this mile. You bring the sign. Cheer loud with Event Day Signs.”
